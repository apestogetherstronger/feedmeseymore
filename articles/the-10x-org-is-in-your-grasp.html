Reader summary

Code generation can get faster while delivery slows down elsewhere. DX reports that organizations where AI generated at least 80% of code waited twice as long for a first review as those where it generated 20–40%. Larger changes and growing review queues are two possible explanations.

Justin Reock proposes giving engineering teams common measures of delivery, quality and developer experience, then letting each team choose what to fix. A team might use review timestamps to locate a delay and ask engineers whether the cause is oversized changes, frequent interruptions or too little time for review.

For a platform team evaluating AI investment, this suggests a concrete question. When a tool saves an engineer an hour writing code, does the change reach users sooner, or does that hour reappear in someone else’s review queue?
